This block breaks down logically for procurement databases:

Product Name: Heavy Hex Structural Bolt — Instantly tells the machinist the shape class.
ASME Dimension Spec: ASME B18.2.6 — Dictates the exact envelope tolerances, hex wrench sizes across flats, and head heights.
Nominal Size & UTS Thread Pitch: he Core Unified Thread Standard (UTS) input.
Product Length: The physical length in inches measured from the underside of the head to the tip.
ASTM Material Grade: Tied to structural material benchmarks (medium carbon or alloy steel hardened to high-tensile specifications)
Protective Coating: Identifies the anti-corrosion layer and its specific thickness standard
